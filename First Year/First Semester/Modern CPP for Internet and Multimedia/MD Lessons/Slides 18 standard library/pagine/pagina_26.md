Modifying sequence algorithms

copy(begin, end, out)
copies the sequence from begin to end to out

unique(begin, end)
removes adjacent duplicates

remove(begin, end, v)
removes elements with value v

replace(begin, end, v, v2)
replaces elements equal to v with value v2
