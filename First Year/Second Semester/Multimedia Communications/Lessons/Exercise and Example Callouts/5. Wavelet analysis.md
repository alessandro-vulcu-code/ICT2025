# 5. Wavelet analysis - Exercise and Example Callouts

> [!Example] EZW Encoding — 4×4 Matrix Example
> **Input DWT coefficients:**
> $$\begin{bmatrix} 26 & 6 & -13 & 10 \\ -7 & 3 & 6 & 4 \\ 4 & -3 & 3 & -3 \\ 2 & -2 & -2 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$
>
> **Bitplane 1 ($T=16$, $n=4$):**
> - Dominant: $|26|=26 \geq 16$ → **SP**; $|6|=6 < 16$, all descendants $\{-13, 10, 6, 4\} < 16$ → **ZR**; $-7$, $3$ also **ZR**
> - Symbol stream: `[SP, ZR, ZR, ZR]`
> - Refining: $26_{10} = 11010_2$ → bit 4 = **1**
> - Stream: `[SP, ZR, ZR, ZR | 1]`
>
> **Bitplane 2 ($T=8$):**
> - Dominant: $6 < 8$ but descendant $13 \geq 8$ → **IZ**; $-7, 3$ → **ZR**; $|-13|=13 \geq 8$ → **SN**; $|10|=10 \geq 8$ → **SP**; remaining < 8 → **ZR**
> - Stream: `[SP,ZR,ZR,ZR | 1 | IZ,ZR,ZR,SN,SP,ZR,ZR]`
> - Refining: $26 \to 0$, $13 \to 1$, $10 \to 0$
> - Stream: `[...|0,1,0]`
>
> **Bitplane 3 ($T=4$):**
> - Dominant: $6 \geq 4$ → SP, $-7 \leq -4$ → SN, $3 < 4$ → ZR; then $6,4,4$ → SP; rest → ZR
> - Refining: bits for all 8 significant coefficients (26→1, 13→0, 10→1, 6→1, 7→1, 6→1, 4→0, 4→0)
>
> **Result:** symbol stream encodes all coefficients progressively, largest first.

---

> [!Example] EZW Decoding — Step by Step
> **After Dominant Pass 1 ($T=16$):** receive SP at [1,1]. MSB known: $1xxxx_2$. Estimate = midpoint of $\{10000_2 \ldots 11111_2\} = 11000_2 = 24$.
>
> **After Refining Pass 1:** receive bit=1. Value now $11xxx_2$. Estimate = midpoint of $\{11000_2 \ldots 11111_2\} = 11100_2 = 28$.
>
> **After Dominant Pass 2 ($T=8$):** receive SN at [1,3]. Estimate = $-01100_2 = -12$.
>
> **After Refining Pass 2:** bits [0,1,0] → 26 becomes 26, -13 becomes -14, 10 stays 10.
>
> Progressive convergence to true values as more bitplanes decoded.
