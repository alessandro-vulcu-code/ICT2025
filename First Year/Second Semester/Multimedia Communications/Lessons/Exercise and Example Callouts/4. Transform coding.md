# 4. Transform coding - Exercise and Example Callouts

> [!Example] Toy Example — Rate-Distortion
> **Without transform** (scalar quantization of $X_1, X_2$):
> Total rate $2R$ → distortion $D = 2\sigma^2 2^{-2R}$
>
> **With transform** (quantize only $Y_1$, skip $Y_2$):
>
> | Bits | Rate $\bar{R}$ | $D_1$ | $D_2$ | $D$ |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | 0 | 0 | $2\sigma^2$ | $\approx 0$ | $2\sigma^2$ |
> | 1 | 0.5 | $\sigma^2/2$ | $\approx 0$ | $\sigma^2/2$ |
> | 2 | 1 | $\sigma^2/8$ | $\approx 0$ | $\sigma^2/8$ |
> | 3 | 1.5 | $\sigma^2/32$ | $\approx 0$ | $\sigma^2/32$ |
>
> Transform coding uses 1 bit per pair = 0.5 bpS but achieves distortion $\sigma^2/2$, far better than scalar quantization at the same rate.

> [!Example] Modified HS — 4 Gaussian Components
> **Data:** $\sigma_1^2=1000,\ \sigma_2^2=100,\ \sigma_3^2=50,\ \sigma_4^2=1$; $R_{\text{Tot}}=10$ bits
>
> **Step 1 — HS on all 4:** GM$= (1000\cdot100\cdot50\cdot1)^{1/4}\approx47.29$
> $$R_k^* = 2.5 + 0.5\log_2\frac{\sigma_k^2}{47.29}: \quad R_1\approx4.70,\ R_2\approx3.04,\ R_3\approx2.54,\ R_4\approx-0.28$$
>
> **Step 2 — Remove $k=4$ (negative):** GM$(1000,100,50)\approx171.00$
> $$R_k = \frac{10}{3}+0.5\log_2\frac{\sigma_k^2}{171}: \quad R_1\approx4.61,\ R_2\approx2.95,\ R_3\approx2.45$$
>
> **Step 3 — Floor:** $R_1=4, R_2=2, R_3=2, R_4=0$ → total=8, residual=2 bits
>
> **Step 4 — Allocate residual** to largest fractional parts ($R_1,R_2$):
> $$R_1=5,\ R_2=3,\ R_3=2,\ R_4=0$$
>
> **Distortions:** $D_1=1000\cdot2^{-10}\approx0.98$, $D_2=100\cdot2^{-6}\approx1.56$, $D_3=50\cdot2^{-4}\approx3.13$, $D_4=1$
> **Total:** $D\approx6.67$

> **Data:** $\sigma_1^2=1000,\ \sigma_2^2=100,\ \sigma_3^2=50,\ \sigma_4^2=1$; $R_{\text{Tot}}=10$
>
> | Iter | $\ell$ | $R_1$ | $R_2$ | $R_3$ | $R_4$ | $D_1$ | $D_2$ | $D_3$ | $D_4$ |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | 0 | — | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1000 | 100 | 50 | 1 |
> | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 250 | 100 | 50 | 1 |
> | 2 | 1 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 62.5 | 100 | 50 | 1 |
> | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 62.5 | 25 | 50 | 1 |
> | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
> | 10 | 1 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 0 | 0.98 | 1.56 | 3.13 | 1 |
>
> Final allocation: $R = [5,3,2,0]$ — identical to modified HS.

> [!Example] DC Encoding: $DC_P = -5$
> Category: $k = \lceil\log_2(|-5|+1)\rceil = 3$ → Huffman code: `100`
> Amplitude: $5 = 101_2$ → complement bits (negative): `010`
> **Total:** `100` + `010` = `100010`
>
> Compare: $DC_P = +5$ → `100` + `101` = `100101`

---

> [!Example] Block Entropy Coding
> Quantized block (after zig-zag scan, assuming $DC_{n-1}=1$):
> $$-3 \;\mid\; (0,17)\; (0,3)\; (1,4)\; (0,3)\; (0,1)\; (5,-1)\; (0,-1)\; (4,-1)\; (5,-1)\; \text{EOB}$$
>
> DC: $DC_P=-3$, cat=2 → `011` + `00` = `01100`
> $(0,17)$: run=0, cat=5 → `11010` + `10001` = `1101010001`
> $(0,3)$: run=0, cat=2 → `01` + `11` = `0111`
> ...
>
> **Final bitstream:** `0110011010100010111111001100011100111101010000111011011101001010`
> → **66 bits for 64 pixels ≈ 1.03 bpp**
