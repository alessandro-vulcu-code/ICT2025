\begin{questionbox}{Domanda 1}
Explain the Contrast Sensitivity Function (CSF): what it is, in which units spatial frequency is measured, where it peaks, and the direct implication for quantization in compression.
\end{questionbox}


\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{CSF}: HVS sensitivity to luminance contrast as spatial frequency changes.
  \item \textbf{Unit}: \textbf{cycles per degree} of visual angle.
  \item \textbf{Shape}: band-pass $\rightarrow$ low sensitivity at very low and very high frequencies; peak at intermediate frequencies.
  \item \textbf{Compression consequence} $\rightarrow$ frequency-dependent quantization:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{visually important frequencies} $\rightarrow$ smaller steps;
    \item \textbf{less visible frequencies} $\rightarrow$ coarser steps.

  \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\begin{questionbox}{Domanda 2}
Compare cones and rods (number, function, lighting conditions) and explain why the RGB-to-Y conversion weights the green component the most.
\end{questionbox}


\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Rods}: high light sensitivity, low-light vision, no color.
  \item \textbf{Cones}: color vision, brighter conditions, short/medium/long wavelength classes.
  \item \textbf{Luminance example}:

\end{itemize}
$$
Y \approx 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B
$$

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Green coefficient largest} $\rightarrow$ HVS most sensitive to green/yellow luminance detail.

\end{itemize}
\begin{questionbox}{Domanda 3}
Explain the J:a:b chroma subsampling notation. Compute the data-reduction factor of 4:2:0 vs full RGB and justify why it is perceptually acceptable.
\end{questionbox}


\begin{itemize}
  \item $J:a:b$ $\rightarrow$ chroma samples kept relative to $J$ luma samples over two rows.
  \item \textbf{4:2:0} $\rightarrow$ full-resolution $Y$, half horizontal and half vertical $Cb,Cr$.
  \item Over $2\times2$ pixels:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RGB}: $4\times3=12$ component samples;
    \item \textbf{YCbCr 4:2:0}: $4Y+1Cb+1Cr=6$ component samples.
  \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Reduction} $\rightarrow$ about $1/2$ of full RGB component samples.
  \item \textbf{Perceptual reason} $\rightarrow$ eye is more sensitive to luminance detail than chrominance detail.

\end{itemize}
