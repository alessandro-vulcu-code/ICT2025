Neural Predictor Architecture

In this experiment a **Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)** has been used:

▶ **Input**: Local causal neighborhood $S = \{A, B, C\}$.
▶ **Function**: $\hat{Y} = f_{MLP}(S, W)$, where $W$ are the learned weights.
▶ **Final Code**: The prediction is rounded to ensure integer residuals.
