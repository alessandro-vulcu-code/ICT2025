Huffman coding

Block coding

▶ Example: coding a B/W image with independent pixels
▶ $\Pr(BB) = p^2$
▶ $\Pr(WB) = p(1 - p) \approx p$
▶ $\Pr(BW) = p(1 - p) \approx p$
▶ $\Pr(WW) = (1 - p)^2 \approx 1 - 2p$
▶ $H(X_1, X_2) = 2H(X) \ll 2$
▶ Huffman coding: $WW \rightarrow 0 \quad WB \rightarrow 10 \quad BW \rightarrow 110 \quad BB \rightarrow 111$
▶ $\mathcal{L} \approx 1$
▶ $\mathcal{L}_S \approx \frac{1}{2}$
