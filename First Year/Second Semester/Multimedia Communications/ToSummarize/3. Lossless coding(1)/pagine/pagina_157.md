Lossless Image Compression: PNG vs. JPEG-LS

Comparison: PNG vs. JPEG-LS

▶ PNG: Uses LZ77 (Dictionary) + Huffman. Better for computer graphics, icons, and text-heavy images. Can use a pixel predictor similar to JPEG-LS

▶ JPEG-LS: Predictive. Better for natural photographs and medical imaging where local gradients follow a statistical model.
