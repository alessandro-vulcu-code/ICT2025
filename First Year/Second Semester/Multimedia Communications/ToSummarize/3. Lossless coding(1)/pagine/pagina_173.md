Residual Histogram & Entropy

Statistical Properties

▶ Distribution: The neural residuals follow a highly peaked Laplacian distribution centered at zero.

▶ Sparsity: The histogram shows that the NN successfully maps the image to a sparse representation.

▶ Entropy Gain: By narrowing the error distribution, the NN reduces the zero-order entropy of residuals, from 2.830 to 2.704 bpp.
