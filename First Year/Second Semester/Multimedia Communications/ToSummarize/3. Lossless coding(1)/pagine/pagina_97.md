Principles of Dictionary-Based Coding

Key Characteristics:

▶ Dynamic Dictionary Construction: The dictionary is built on-the-fly during the encoding process
▶ Universality: No prior knowledge of source statistics or probability distributions is required
▶ Adaptivity: It automatically tracks and adapts to non-stationary signals where statistics change over time
▶ Practical Efficiency: It is the fundamental principle behind ubiquitous formats like ZIP, GZIP, etc.
▶ Why use it? While Entropy coding (Huffman, arithmetic) struggles with very high-order dependencies (large $K$), dictionary-based methods scale efficiently by identifying long redundant patterns
