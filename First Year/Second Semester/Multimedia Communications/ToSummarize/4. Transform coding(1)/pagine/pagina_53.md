Towards the Optimal Transform

▶ In our Toy Example, we saw that applying an orthogonal transform (a rotation) to highly correlated variables successfully decorrelated them.

▶ This decorrelation caused a strong sparsification: the energy was packed into one main component, drastically reducing the GM of the variances.
