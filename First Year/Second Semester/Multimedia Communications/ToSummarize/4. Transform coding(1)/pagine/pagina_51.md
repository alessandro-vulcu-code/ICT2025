Recap: Transform Coding and Coding Gain

The Role of Variances

With block coding and optimal resource allocation (Huang-Schulteiss or greedy), the distortion depends on the Geometric Mean (GM) of the component variances: $D^* \propto \sigma_{GM}^2$

Applying an Orthogonal Transform $Y = \mathcal{T}X$:

▶ Preserves energy: The Arithmetic Mean (AM) of the variances remains constant $(\sigma_{AM,Y}^2 = \sigma_{AM,X}^2)$.

▶ Alters sparsity: It changes the distribution of the energy among the coefficients, directly affecting the GM.
