Frequency analysis as sparsification

Left. A vector $x$ of $M$ samples from a sound signal (pure tone). The signal is non-sparse as all the samples are equally important

Right. The “transformed” vector $y = Ax$, where $A$ is an orthogonal matrix allowing to perform a frequency analysis. The resulting signal is sparse: one non-negligible sample and $N - 1$ negligible samples.

In general a sparse signal is made up of “a few” non-negligible samples and “many” negligible samples
