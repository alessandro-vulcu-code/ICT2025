DFT vs DCT

In conclusion, applying the DCT to a signal (a sequence of $M$ real numbers) produces $M$ real coefficients and has a better sparsification property than DFT thanks to the symmetric periodization.

For these reasons, DFT is never used for compression, while DCT is very common.
