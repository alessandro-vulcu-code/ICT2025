Modified H.S.: Example

▶ Initial R values:

$$R(1) \approx 4.70 \quad R(2) \approx 3.04$$
$$R(3) \approx 2.54 \quad R(4) \approx -0.28$$

▶ The rates are not all positive: we remove the variance corresponding to the minimum (most negative) rate, set its rate to zero, and continue with $M - 1$ variances

▶ GM $(1000, 100, 50) \approx 171.00$

▶ H.S. formula on active variances:

$$R(k) = \frac{10}{3} + 0.5 \log_2 \frac{\sigma_k^2}{171.00}$$
