Proof of the Huang-Schulteiss formula

$$R_{\text{Tot}} = \sum_{k=1}^{M} R_k^* = M\lambda' + \frac{1}{2} \sum_{k=1}^{M} \log_2 \left( c_k \sigma_k^2 \right)$$

$$\lambda' = \frac{R_{\text{Tot}}}{M} - \frac{1}{2M} \sum_{k=1}^{M} \log_2 \left( c_k \sigma_k^2 \right)$$

$$\lambda' = \frac{R_{\text{Tot}}}{M} - \frac{1}{2} \log_2 \left( c_{\text{GM}} \sigma_{\text{GM}}^2 \right)$$

Now, replacing $\lambda'$ in the expression of $R_k^*$, we find:

$$R_k^* = \lambda' + \frac{1}{2} \log_2 \left( c_k \sigma_k^2 \right) = \frac{R_{\text{Tot}}}{M} - \frac{1}{2} \log_2 \left( c_{\text{GM}} \sigma_{\text{GM}}^2 \right) + \frac{1}{2} \log_2 \left( c_k \sigma_k^2 \right)$$

$$= \frac{R_{\text{Tot}}}{M} + \frac{1}{2} \log_2 \left( \frac{c_k \sigma_k^2}{c_{\text{GM}} \sigma_{\text{GM}}^2} \right)$$
