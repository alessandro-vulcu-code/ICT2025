DFT basis

Real and imaginary parts of each basis vector $B_k$, without normalization for better visualization.

DFT’s k-th coefficient tells us how much vector $X$ is similar to the k-th basis vector.

Geometrically, the DFT represents a rigid rotation in the complex vector space $\mathbb{C}^N$.

It preserves the $\ell_2$-norm, i.e. the energy: $\|Y\| = \|X\|$.
