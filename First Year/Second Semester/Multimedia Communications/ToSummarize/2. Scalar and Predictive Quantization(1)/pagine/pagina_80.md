High Resolution (HR) Uniform Quantization

$$\text{SNR} = 10 \log_{10} \frac{\text{E}\{X^2\}}{D} = 10 \log_{10} \frac{\sigma_X^2}{A^2/12} 2^{2R} \approx 6R - 10 \log_{10} \frac{\gamma^2}{3}$$

where $\gamma^2 = \frac{X_X^2}{\sigma_X^2} = \frac{A^2/4}{\sigma_X^2}$ is the load factor, i.e. the ratio between the peak power and the average power.

$$D = \frac{A^2}{3} 2^{-2R} = \frac{\gamma^2}{3} \sigma_X^2 2^{-2R} = K_X \sigma_X^2 2^{-2R}$$
