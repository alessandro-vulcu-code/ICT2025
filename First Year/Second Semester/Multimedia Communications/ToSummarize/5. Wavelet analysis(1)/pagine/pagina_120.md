Algorithm

JPEG2000 is made up of two *tiers*

▶ First tier
  ▶ DWT and quantization
  ▶ Lossless coding of codeblocks

▶ Second tier
  ▶ EBCOT: embedded block coding with optimized truncation
  ▶ Scalability (quality, resolution) and ROI management
