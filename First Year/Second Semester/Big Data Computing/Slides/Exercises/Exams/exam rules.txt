Written exam (28 points):

    The written exam consists of a number of theory questions and exercises to be answered/solved in 2.5 hours.
    There will be one written exam administered in each of the four exam sessions.
    The written exam is taken individually by each student.
    In order to pass the exam, a student must get at least 16 points in the written exam and 18 points as a sum of the homeworks/project and written exam evaluations.

In the zipped folder ExamExamples.zip there are 5 examples of written tests from previous years. Please take into account that in previous years the written exam contributed 26 points and its duration was 2 hours, while this year it contributes 28 points and its duration is 2.5 hours. This year tests will have the same same structure as the examples but (possibly) a slightly increased length. Also, some questions regard previous years' homeworks, while questions on homeworks in the upcoming exams will clearly concern this year's homeworks.
