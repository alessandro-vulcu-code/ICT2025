Conclusions: EZW/JP2K conc. map

Wavelet Transform
Advantages:
- Better model of natural images
- Variable freq/time resolution
- Energy compaction (sparsity)

Bit-plane Coding
Advantages:
- Quality Scalability
- RD-optimal
- Lossy-to-Lossless

Coding Strategies
JPEG 2000 (Tiers 1/2, EBCOT)
Codeblocks + R-D Optimization (Lagrange)
EZW (Embedded Zerotrees)
Exploits inter-scale Correlation: Zero-tree Roots (ZR)

Comparison with JPEG
+ High quality @ low rates
+ No blocking artifacts
+ ROI coding (JP2K)
- Lower (EZW) or slightly higher (JP2K) complexity
