Mechanism: Shift and Dilation

▶ Large $a$ (Dilation): The wavelet stretches in time and shrinks in frequency (lower frequencies, finer $\Delta f$).

▶ Small $a$ (Compression): The wavelet shrinks in time and stretches in frequency (higher frequencies, finer $\Delta t$).
