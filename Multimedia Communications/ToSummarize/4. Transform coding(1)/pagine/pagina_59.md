Energy Compaction and Sparsity

▶ **Sparsifying Property**: Most transformed coefficients $Y_i$ for large $i$ will have near-zero variance.

▶ **Dimension Reduction**: We can discard (or coarsely quantize) components with small eigenvalues with minimal impact on total Mean Squared Error (MSE).

**The Packing Efficiency**

Among all linear orthogonal transforms, the KLT is **optimal** for energy compaction: for any $N < M$, the sum of the first $N$ variances is maximized by the KLT.
