KLT: 3D Visual Intuition

3D Decorrelation and Sparsification

The KLT rotates the 3D space to align the axes with the cluster’s principal directions. It packs the variance into the first components, effectively "squashing" the data distribution along the minor axes.

▶ **Alignment**: The KLT rotates the coordinate system to align the axes with the principal directions of the data distribution (the axes of the "ellipse").

▶ **Intuition**: In the $Y$ domain, knowing the value of $Y_1$ provides zero linear information about $Y_2$. The redundancy present in the "diagonal" alignment is removed.
