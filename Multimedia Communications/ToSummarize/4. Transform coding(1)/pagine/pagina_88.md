DCT examples

The top left coefficient of the DCT (image on the right) is the “DC component” i.e. the zero-frequency component of the signal. The other coefficients are called AC and represent the direction (angle) and the velocity of spatial variations of the signal (slow variation = low frequency, fast variation –e.g., contours– = high frequencies).

In this example we see two dominant directions of the contours. This corresponds to the two “traces” in the DCT image
