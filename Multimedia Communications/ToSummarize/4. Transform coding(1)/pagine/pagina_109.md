JPEG Standard: Quantization

- Uniform quantization (mid-tread)
- $\tilde{c}_{i,j} = \text{round}\left(\frac{c_{i,j}}{q_{i,j}}\right)$
- The quantization table $q$ drives the rate-distortion trade-off, but it is not defined by the standard
- Thus $q$ must be encoded
