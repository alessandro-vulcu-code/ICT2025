Transform coding: Toy Example

We apply the transform: $\begin{bmatrix} Y_1 \\ Y_2 \end{bmatrix} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} X_1 \\ X_2 \end{bmatrix}$

After the transform the RV are independent

$$f_{Y_1, Y_2}(y_1, y_2) = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{\Delta_1 \Delta_2} & \text{if } (y_1, y_2) \in S \\ 0 & \text{if } (y_1, y_2) \notin S \end{cases}$$

$$Y_1 \sim U \left[ -\frac{\Delta_1}{2}, \frac{\Delta_1}{2} \right]$$

$$Y_2 \sim U \left[ -\frac{\Delta_2}{2}, \frac{\Delta_2}{2} \right]$$
