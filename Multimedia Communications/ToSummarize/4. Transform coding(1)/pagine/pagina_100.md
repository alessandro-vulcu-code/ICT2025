Summary

1. **Block coding**: take a joint decision on the quantization rate of a block of data, looking for better RD (rate-distortion) performance than the case of sample-by-sample quantization
2. **Huang-Schulteiss formula**: gives the best coding rate for each component of a random vector $X$
3. **Coding gain**: shows that total distortion is proportional to the geometric mean of the component variances, $\sigma_{\text{GM}}^2$
4. **Orthogonal transforms**: allow to modify (reduce) $\sigma_{\text{GM}}^2$
5. **Practical rate allocation**: modified H.S., greedy allocation, fixed allocation can all be used in practical cases; first 2 are equivalent, third one is simpler and often used
6. **Frequency transforms**: convenient special cases of orthogonal transforms
