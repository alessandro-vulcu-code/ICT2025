Constrained optimization: Lagrange’s method

Let’s consider a function $f : x \in \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$
In order to find the maximum or minimum of a function $f(x)$ subjected to the equality constraint $\phi(x) = 0$, we look for the stationary points of the functional

$$J(x, \lambda) = f(x) + \lambda \cdot \phi(x)$$

with $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$. The solution is among these points.
The stationary points are computed by setting to zero all the derivatives of $J$:

$$\frac{\partial J}{\partial x_i} = 0 \quad \frac{\partial J}{\partial \lambda} = 0$$
