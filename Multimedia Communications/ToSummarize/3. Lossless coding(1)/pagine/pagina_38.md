Conditional entropy

We consider a couple of r.v. $X$ and $Y$
Let $p_j = \mathbb{P}\{Y = y_j\}$
Conditional entropy:

$$H(X|Y) = \sum_j p_j H(X|Y = y_j)$$

It is easy to show that:

$$H(X, Y) = H(Y) + H(X|Y)$$
$$H(X) + H(Y|X)$$
