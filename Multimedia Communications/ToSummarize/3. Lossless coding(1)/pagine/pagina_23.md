Proof of Necessity

The total number of blocked leaves cannot exceed the available leaves: $\sum_{i=1}^{N} 2^{L_{\max}-\ell_i} \leq 2^{L_{\max}}$. Dividing by $2^{L_{\max}}$ yields the Kraft inequality: $\sum_{i=1}^{N} 2^{-\ell_i} \leq 1$
