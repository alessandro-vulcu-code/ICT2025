Distribution with maximum entropy

Constrained maximization problem:

$$p^* = \arg \max_p \sum_{i=1}^{M} p_i \log \frac{1}{p_i}$$

$$\sum_{i=1}^{M} p_i = 1$$

In order to solve this constrained optimization problem, we need a specific tool: the Lagrange’s method (or method of Lagrange’s multipliers)
