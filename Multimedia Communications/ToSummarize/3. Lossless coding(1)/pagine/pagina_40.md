Conditional Entropy

Formal Definition

▶ The average uncertainty about $X$ given $Y$: $H(X|Y) = \sum_j p_j H(X|Y = y_j)$

▶ The Chain Rule: $H(X, Y) = H(Y) + H(X|Y)$

What does it represent?

▶ Residual Uncertainty: The amount of information still required to describe $X$ after $Y$ has been observed.

▶ Equivocation: In communications, it is the information lost due to a noisy channel.

If $X$ and $Y$ are independent, $H(X|Y) = H(X)$.
If $X$ is a deterministic function of $Y$, $H(X|Y) = 0$.
