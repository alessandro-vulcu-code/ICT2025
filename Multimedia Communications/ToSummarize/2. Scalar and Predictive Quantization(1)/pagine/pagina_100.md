$$X(n) \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$$
$$V(n) = X(n-1)$$

$$\text{E}[X(n)X(m)] = \sigma^2\rho^{|n-m|}$$

$$\rho : G_P > 0 ?$$

$$Y(n) = X(n) - X(n-1)$$ Zero mean Gaussian RV

$$\sigma_Y^2 = \text{E}[(X(n) - X(n-1))^2] = 2\sigma^2 - 2\sigma^2\rho$$

$$G_P = 10 \log_{10} \frac{\sigma_X^2}{\sigma_Y^2} = 10 \log_{10} \frac{\sigma^2}{2(1-\rho)\sigma^2}$$

$$G_P > 0 \iff \rho > \frac{1}{2}$$
