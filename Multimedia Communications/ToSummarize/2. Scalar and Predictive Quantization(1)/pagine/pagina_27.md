# Mathematical Reminders: Floor Function

Operator: $[x]$

- **Definition**: The greatest integer less than or equal to $x$
- **Behavior**: Always rounds towards $-\infty$
- **Examples**:
  - $[2.7] = 2$
  - $[-2.7] = -3$
- **Usage**: Common in basic uniform quantization models
