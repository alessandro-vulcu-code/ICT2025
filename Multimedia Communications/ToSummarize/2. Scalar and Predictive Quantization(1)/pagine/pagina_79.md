High Resolution (HR) Uniform Quantization

▶ Hypothesis: $L \rightarrow +\infty$, $X$ generic RV
▶ In HR, in any given $\Theta^i$ we approximate $p_X$ as a constant (it is constant in the region, but the value can be different in another region)
▶ Therefore, the quantization noise in $\Theta^i$ is $U\left(-\frac{\Delta}{2}, \frac{\Delta}{2}\right)$
▶ From the total probability law, $E \sim U\left(-\frac{\Delta}{2}, \frac{\Delta}{2}\right)$
▶ Thus:

$$D = \frac{\Delta^2}{12} = \frac{A^2}{12} 2^{-2R}$$
