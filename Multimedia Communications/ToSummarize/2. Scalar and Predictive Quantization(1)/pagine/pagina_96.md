Sparse Signals: a preview

Definition: A signal is sparse if most of its elements are zero or close to zero
If a sample is close to zero, it can be “neglected” (quantized as zero) without introducing large distortion
The variance of a sparse signal is low
Natural signals are not sparse in their original form, but could be after some processing
The main tools for signal sparsification are prediction and linear transform
