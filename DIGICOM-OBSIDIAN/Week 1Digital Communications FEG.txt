The di ferential entropy rate does not measure the information contained in a
random variable.
If we only have a random variable, we only have a shot. In order to consider a
rate, we consider a random process: in this case, we have a random variable every
certain amount of time: we can say that we have a certain amount of information
per second!

A binary random process with p=0.5 which gives a value every second, gives 1
bit/s.

So the rate does not measure the information contained in a random variable.
We also need to keep in mind that the di ferential entropy can be negative.

It's easy to prove that a Gaussian distribution maximizes the di ferential
entropy, for a fixed variance.
